OUR STORY

How Lavender Loops began

Angelykah's Lavender Loops started from a simple love for crochet and handmade crafts. Her first product was a Crocheted Bralette. What began as her hobby slowly became a small business where creativity could be shared with others.


Every product is handmade by Angelykah carefully and thoughtfully. We believe that handmade gifts are special because they carry the time, effort, and love of the person who created them.


Our goal is to continue creating beautiful crochet pieces that bring happiness to our customers.
